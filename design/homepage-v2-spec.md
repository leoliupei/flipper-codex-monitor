# Homepage pixel draft v2

- Native canvas remains `128x64`, monochrome, with no antialiasing.
- The decorative logo has been removed. `CODEX` begins at `x=2`.
- The left footer is now a countdown: `RESET IN HHH:MM`.
- Three hour digits support the entire weekly reset range through `167:59`.
- The displayed `167:52` is a live-format example captured on 2026-08-08.
- The right footer is shortened to `1 RESET`; the word is complete and unambiguous.
- `RESET IN 167:52` occupies 59 pixels and ends at `x=60`.
- `1 RESET` occupies 27 pixels and begins at `x=99`.
- The two footer regions therefore retain a 38-pixel visual gap.
- No FAP source or installed application is changed by this draft.
